The people and work behind it

A little recognition.

The original world

Arcanum: Of Steamworks & Magick Obscura was created by Troika Games and published by Sierra Entertainment. Its name, artwork, characters and native sound effects belong to their respective rights holders. The screenshots and films show the mod running with the original game; original game data is required separately and is not included in the downloads.

The engine

ArcanumMP is a modified fork of Arcanum Community Edition by alexbatalov and contributors, distributed free of charge for noncommercial use under the same Sustainable Use License 1.0. The source and download bundles retain the upstream notices. Third-party libraries retain their own licenses.

The films

Four actual native Linux game instances connected over TCP were used for multiplayer footage. Characters, equipment, statistics, encounters and travel were prepared for filming. The feature demonstrations use the native controls and current interface. These are edited, staged encounters, rather than a continuous normal-party campaign or a balance test.

Titles, crops and split views are editorial layers. Gameplay frames were recorded from the engine; multiplayer panels and gameplay were not generated. Extra transition sounds were created for the edit. Native spell and interface effects are from the original game.

Music

“Arcadia” and “Five Armies” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com). Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0. The recordings were excerpted, mixed, faded and normalized for these films.

Type and interface art

The site's Cormorant Garamond typeface is by the Cormorant Project Authors, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original and generated project HUD artwork, reference images and prompts are documented in the source repository. The trailer's FreeSerif and Liberation typefaces remain under their respective font licenses; they are rendered into the film, not embedded as font files.

ArcanumMP is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original game's rights holders or the credited composers.
